COTTON HIGHLIGHTS

COTTON: Offsetting adjustments in U.S. cotton
consumption and trade leave 1995/96 ending stocks
unchanged from last month at 2.9 million bales.
While imports are revised up 100,000 bales to
300,000, exports are raised 200,000 bales to 7.6
million.  Mill use is revised down 100,000 bales
to 10.4 million.  Slightly lower production of
17.9 million bales reflects the recent Cotton
Ginnings report.

The world cotton outlook for 1995/96 is
highlighted this month by slightly smaller
production and modestly larger trade.  Output is
pegged at 88.8 million bales, down 200,000 bales
from March indications, as a smaller crop in
Pakistan more than offsets larger Indian
production.  Global consumption is revised down
100,000 bales this month, while trade is revised
up nearly 1 percent.  This season's ending stocks
are projected at 31.8 million bales, about
unchanged from a month earlier.
