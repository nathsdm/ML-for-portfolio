COTTON HIGHLIGHTS

Larger production, use and stocks highlight this
month’s U.S. cotton outlook for 1995/96.  The 1995 crop
is forecast at a record 21.8 million bales, 1 percent
above the July projection and 11 percent above last
year's record output.  With the larger supply,
projected mill use and exports are raised slightly to
11.5 and 7.7 million bales, respectively.  This
season’s carryover is projected at 5.2 million bales, 4
percent above the month-earlier indication and more
than double the revised beginning stock level.

World cotton prospects for 1995/96 are about unchanged
this month.  Forecast production of 90.8 million bales
matches last month as the larger U.S. crop offsets a
downward revision for the former Soviet Union.  Global
consumption and trade are slightly above July
indications.  This season’s ending stocks are placed at
34 million bales, near the month-earlier level but 14
percent above the carryin.

APPROVED:


/s/ Richard E. Rominger
