COTTON HIGHLIGHTS                                                               
                                                                                
COTTON: The U.S. cotton outlook for 1995/96 is                                  
highlighted this month by slightly smaller stocks                               
stemming from an upward adjustment in exports.                                  
Exports are revised up 3 percent to 7.4 million                                 
bales, reflecting further recent sales and                                      
continuing strong weekly shipments.  U.S. imports                               
also are increased to 200,000 bales.  Mill                                      
consumption remains unchanged at 10.5 million                                   
bales.  The 1995/96 carryover is projected at 2.9                               
million bales, 3 percent below February                                         
indications and equal to 16 percent of total use.                               
                                                                                
This month?s world cotton prospects for 1995/96                                 
feature modest increases in production,                                         
consumption, and trade.  The production forecast                                
of 89 million bales is up 400,000 bales as larger                               
crops in China, Australia, and Uzbekistan more                                  
than offset smaller production in Turkmenistan and                              
Brazil.  With larger use in India, global                                       
consumption is raised slightly to nearly 86                                     
million bales.  In addition, world trade is up                                  
about 1 percent from a month earlier. This                                      
season?s ending stocks are projected at 31.7                                    
million bales, virtually unchanged from February                                
indications and 10 percent above the beginning                                  
level.
